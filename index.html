    Questions, answered.

    What is a rebound boots class?

    A 60-minute fitness dancing class on rebound boots, also called jumping boots or bounce boots. The springs under your feet turn every step into a bounce, set to Latin, Masry and remix music.

    Is it suitable for beginners?

    Yes. Every class uses basic movements and you grow through three levels: Beginner (find your bounce), Intermediate (build your power) and Advanced (let it fly). Erika guides every step.

    Do I need my own boots?

    No. Erika’s boots come in sizes S (EU 36–37), M (EU 38–39) and L (EU 40–42), and you choose your size when you book. You can also bring your own, or order a pair from Erika from 16,000 EGP.

    How much does a bounce experience cost?

    In December 2026 every bounce experience class is 500 EGP instead of 850 EGP, a special price for a limited time. Boots are included.

    When is the next bounce experience?

    Tuesday 1 December 2026 at 7 PM Cairo time, 60 minutes at Heliopolis Sporting Club, for Heliopolis Club members only. More bounce experiences follow through December 2026, each in a new place. See the calendar, and join our community to hear first.

    Where are the bounce experiences?

    Every bounce experience happens in a new place, with new people. For now we have no fixed studio: through December 2026 we bounce in different places in Cairo, starting at Heliopolis Sporting Club. Join our community to follow our bouncing steps.

    How do I book and pay?

    Pick your boots size on the booking page to reserve your spot, then pay with InstaPay and share the payment screenshot on WhatsApp. Once the payment is checked, you receive a confirmation by email and WhatsApp.

    Who is Erika?

    Erika Szenes is a Hungarian coach with more than 20 years in fitness and wellness. She grew up in choreography and ballet in Hungary, found her rhythm on rebound boots, and has spent 15+ years bringing her experience from Hungary to Egypt.

    What should I wear and bring?

    Comfortable sportswear, socks and a bottle of water. Your boots are ready for you if you booked a pair.

    Which languages does Erika coach in?

    English, Hungarian and Arabic.
