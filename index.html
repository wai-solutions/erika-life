01
Beginner

Find your bounce

Soft knees, steady core, gentle springs.

Soft BounceSmall, gentle bounces. Feel the springs.
Bounce MarchShift from boot to boot in rhythm.
Heel TapTap one heel forward, then switch.
Side StepStep side to side with a light bounce.

Intermediate

Build your power

Bigger lifts and new directions.

Knee LiftLift one knee, bounce on the other boot.
Swing KickHop side to side with a straight-leg kick.

Before your first bounce

Rebound boots, also called jumping boots or bounce boots, are fitness boots with a curved spring shell under each foot. Every step presses the springs down and they lift you back up, so you can run, dance and jump with soft landings.

A 60-minute fitness dancing class on rebound boots, led by Erika. It mixes a warm-up, basic movements, a dance combo to Masry, Latin and remix music, a power finish and a cool-down.

Erika teaches in New Cairo on Sunday and Tuesday evenings and in Heliopolis on Monday and Wednesday evenings, with classes at 6, 7 and 8 PM.

Erika Szenes is a Hungarian international fitness coach and former dancer with more than 20 years in fitness and wellness and over 16 years on rebound boots. She coaches in English, Hungarian and Arabic and has lived in Cairo since 2012.
